# v0.2.29 release notes — 2026-10-16

**Your two UX asks, plus the receipts foundation hardening.**

## What's new

- **HOST now shows it's starting.** Pressing the hosting switch
  immediately animates "STARTING…" with a glowing switch and a sweeping
  bar for the whole engine-boot window — no more silent seconds — then
  settles to the truthful ON/OFF. Turning it off shows "STOPPING…"
  the same way.
- **The resource monitor is back on the main page.** Three always-on
  horizontal bars (CPU · RAM · GPU, 1 Hz, live values) sit right under
  the Host section. The old Help-menu panel and its hide button are
  gone — nothing to hide.
- **Under the hood (ADR-030 B2):** every cleanly-served swarm request
  now carries a cryptographically signed work receipt on the wire —
  the foundation for verified-work accounting, fair queueing, and
  reputation. Invisible today by design: the requester-side consumption
  ships next, when the first accounting entries appear.

## For testers

Installers remain unsigned by owner decision. Self-update: 0.2.28
clients pick this up automatically on the start/six-hour check.
